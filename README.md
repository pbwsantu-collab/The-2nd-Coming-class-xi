🌙 The Second Coming — Class XI Interactive MCQ Engine
An interactive, exam-focused English learning app based on W. B. Yeats's The Second Coming.
A single-file HTML learning engine containing 100 clickable MCQs, instant feedback, marks, progress tracking, English-to-Bengali vocabulary, clickable study notes, and a delightful flower-shower animation 🌸 for correct answers.
✨ Why This Project?
Designed for Class XI students, especially learners preparing for textual and objective-type examinations.
The engine combines:
📖 Literature + 🧠 MCQ Practice + 🌐 English–Bengali Vocabulary + 🎯 Instant Assessment + ✨ Animation
Everything works inside one HTML file.

🧠 Important Concepts Covered
Students should be particularly familiar with:
🦅 Falcon & Falconer
Represents the breakdown of communication/control between a force and the authority that once controlled it.
🌀 Widening Gyre
Connected with Yeats's theory of historical cycles and increasing separation from the centre.
⚖️ Centre
Represents stabilizing moral, social and spiritual forces.
🌊 Blood-Dimmed Tide
Suggests violence, destruction and historical bloodshed.
🕊️ Ceremony of Innocence
Suggests moral purity and established civilized values.
👁️ Sphinx-Like Creature
A mysterious and frightening image representing an emerging historical force.
🐾 Rough Beast
Represents the terrifying new age that appears to be approaching.
⭐ Bethlehem
Creates a powerful connection with the traditional Christian expectation of Christ's birth/Second Coming.
⚠️ Educational Note
This application is designed as a study and revision aid. It should be used alongside the prescribed textbook and classroom teaching.
The MCQs focus on interpretation, literary analysis and examination preparation; literary interpretation can sometimes allow more than one defensible reading.
